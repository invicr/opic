### 🗣️ 답변

Hmm, let me think for a second... Oh, I still remember the first time I went to a cafe. It was back when I was in college.
음, 잠깐 생각해볼게요... 아, 처음 카페에 갔던 게 아직도 기억나요. 대학생 때였어요.

So, my friend ordered an Americano, and you know, I just ordered the same thing without really thinking.
친구가 아메리카노를 시키길래, 별 생각 없이 저도 똑같이 시켰어요.

But honestly, when I took the first sip, it was so bitter and I really didn't like it at all.
근데 솔직히 첫 모금을 마셨을 때 너무 써서 전혀 맛이 없었어요.

And on top of that, I was really shocked by how expensive it was!
게다가 가격이 너무 비싸서 엄청 놀랐죠!

I remember thinking, why would anyone pay this much for something this bitter?
이렇게 쓴 걸 왜 이 돈 주고 마시지? 라고 생각했던 게 기억나요.

But you know, it's funny how things change — now it's actually my favorite place to go!
근데 웃긴 건, 지금은 제가 가장 자주 가는 카페가 됐다는 거예요!

I guess my taste has changed a lot since then!
그때랑 입맛이 많이 달라진 것 같아요!
