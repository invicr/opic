### 🗣️ 답변

Um, so I have this really frustrating memory about a restaurant reservation.
음, 레스토랑 예약 관련해서 정말 황당했던 기억이 있어요.

Like, I made a reservation for 3 people at 7pm, you know.
3명으로 저녁 7시에 예약을 했었거든요.

But when we got there, they told us there was no reservation under my name — I was so confused!
근데 도착했더니 제 이름으로 예약이 없다는 거예요, 너무 당황스러웠어요!

So I asked them to double-check, but like, they still said nothing was there.
다시 확인해달라고 했는데, 그래도 없다고 하더라고요.

I mean, I couldn't believe it — I was so annoyed at that point!
진짜 믿기지 않았어요, 그때 너무 짜증났어요!

So we just had to find another place nearby, which was kind of a bummer.
결국 근처 다른 식당을 찾아갔는데, 좀 실망스러웠죠.

So yeah, I always double-check my reservations now — lesson learned!
그래서 이제는 항상 예약을 두 번씩 확인해요, 교훈을 얻었죠!
